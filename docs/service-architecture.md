# Cleaning and moving: current structure

The London pilot site offers Cleaning and Moving only. Gas, electrical and plumbing work are outside its scope.

## Implemented routes

- / — two linked category image cards and all 12 subservice summaries, visible together.
- /cleaning — six cleaning services with stable heading anchors and enquiry links.
- /moving — six moving services with stable heading anchors and enquiry links.

The navigation mega menu and visible homepage lists link to category pages at the relevant subservice anchor. There is one content source, src/content/services.ts, for the category names, service names and descriptions.

## Future subservice routes — not created yet

Cleaning: domestic-cleaning, deep-cleaning, end-of-tenancy-cleaning, carpet-upholstery-cleaning, oven-appliance-cleaning, office-cleaning.

Moving: house-removals, flat-removals, man-and-van, packing-unpacking, furniture-moving, office-removals.

Create separate pages only once each service is confirmed and there is useful distinct detail about its scope, exclusions, access requirements, process and common questions. No keyword-volume research or ranking claims are implied by the proposed list.

End-of-tenancy cleaning can be linked from the moving journey without duplicating its future main service page. Customers can request both categories in one enquiry.

## Launch requirements

Confirm providers, offered subservices, postcode coverage and privacy/contact content; connect real enquiry handling; then enable indexing. The current form does not send or store details.
