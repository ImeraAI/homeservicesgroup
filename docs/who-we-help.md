# Who We Help

Four visible cards follow the supplied reference: Homeowners & tenants, Private landlords, Agents & property managers, and Businesses & workspaces. Each has a description, three cleaning/moving examples and an audience-specific enquiry button. The section keeps the white background and green/navy branding.

The buttons lead to the existing enquiry form, not extra audience pages. They preselect the optional audience field; the tenancy-cleaning CTA also selects end-of-tenancy cleaning. Other cards leave service choice open. Both fields remain editable. No enquiry is transmitted or stored by the demo form.

Validation: production build and TypeScript passed. Browser checks verified all four CTA audience selections, the tenancy-cleaning default and a 390px mobile layout with no horizontal overflow. Mobile CTA heights are 50px. Screenshot: who-we-help-preview.png.
